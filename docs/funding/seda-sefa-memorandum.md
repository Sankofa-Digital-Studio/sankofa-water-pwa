## 1. Executive Summary & Company Profile

Sankofa Digital Labs is an indigenous South African civic tech and embedded IoT systems engineering venture headquartered in Welkom. The enterprise has engineered **"The Boundary Watch"** — a proprietary low-power telemetry node (R620 BOM cost) that non-invasively connects to boundary water meter boxes, detects catastrophic 12+ bar water hammer transients, and pinpoints subterranean bursts across Matjhabeng's deteriorating asbestos-cement water infrastructure.

### The Problem in Matjhabeng
- 64.2% Non-Revenue Water (NRW) loss across 1,540 km of municipal pipelines.
- Over R656 million in treated drinking water lost underground annually.
- Outstanding municipal debt of over R6.0B to Vaal Central Water and R5.8B to Eskom.
- Deep underground bursts (4 to 6 meters) go unnoticed until road sinkholes appear or commercial operations stall.
